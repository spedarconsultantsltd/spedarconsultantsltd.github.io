How an assignment takes shape

A transparent, practical process informed by established consultancy practice, adapted to the size and complexity of each brief.

  1. 01

    Define

    Clarify the question, intended users, timing and outputs.

  2. 02

    Scope

    Agree methods, required inputs, responsibilities and milestones.

  3. 03

    Analyse

    Collect or process evidence and check data quality.

  4. 04

    Review

    Share draft findings, limitations and interpretation for discussion.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Provide agreed outputs, documentation and handover guidance.

QUALITY & TRANSPARENCY

Clarity is part of the deliverable.

A well-structured consultancy output should make it possible to understand where the evidence came from, what was checked, and what remains uncertain.

Source transparency

Clearly record data provenance and analysis assumptions.

Quality checks

Plan proportionate checks and review steps for the assignment.

Useful communication

Tailor maps, visuals and reports to the intended audience.

Honest boundaries

Confirm specialist credentials and any independent approvals where a scope requires them.

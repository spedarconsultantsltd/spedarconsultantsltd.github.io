Our team

Twelve professionals in six practice units. Members contribute to assignments according to the expertise each project requires.

Division heads and leads report to the CEO and Managing Director. Junior officers and interns work within their practice units.

Executive leadership

Sets strategy, leads client engagement and proposals, and signs off final deliverables. All practice units report to the CEO and Managing Director.

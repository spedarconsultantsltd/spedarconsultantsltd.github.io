CLIENT & COLLABORATOR GUIDANCE

Questions worth asking before a project begins.

Practical answers about service scope, technical qualifications, deliverables and how to get in touch.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear expectations from the beginning.

Transparency matters. The service directory is intentionally broad, while assignments are accepted only after scope and capacity are confirmed.

What kinds of assignments can SPEDAR discuss?

Project-based enquiries involving GIS, planning research, environmental analysis, field surveys, software and communications can be discussed with the team. Broader specialist fields are screened for appropriate collaborators and qualifications before acceptance.

Are all 226 services provided directly by the current team?

No. The service directory is an enquiry and scoping catalogue, not an unqualified promise. Every topic is marked as a team-aligned capability or specialist scoping. Engineering sign-off, regulated assessments and specialist equipment must be confirmed for a specific engagement.

Can we share a Terms of Reference (ToR) or Request for Proposal?

Yes. Send a brief outline or share a document link through the project enquiry builder, then attach the ToR in your email. The website does not collect or store attachments.

How does an assignment begin?

We begin with an initial conversation about the problem, available evidence, geographic scope, timeline, data, outputs and required professional credentials. A proposal or quotation is prepared only after a feasible scope has been discussed.

Does the online enquiry form send data automatically?

No. This is a static GitHub Pages site. The enquiry builder prepares an email draft in the visitor’s own email application; the visitor must review and send that email. A successful email delivery is not claimed by the website.

Can SPEDAR work with government, development organisations and universities?

These organisations are potential collaboration audiences. Any proposed work depends on eligibility, procurement requirements, capacity, contracting arrangements and other applicable conditions.

Do you carry out licensed design, ESIA, or specialist hydrographic surveys?

These are specialist-scoping categories. The correct professional registration, equipment, permissions, independent sign-off or project partner must first be identified and confirmed. If the requirements cannot be met, the assignment should not be accepted.

What outputs might we receive?

Depending on scope, outputs may include maps, spatial data, research tools, survey datasets, analytical reports, dashboards, presentations, technical notes or an agreed handover package. Final deliverables are confirmed in writing.

Are sample sectors and service areas examples of completed SPEDAR projects?

No. Sector pages explain possible applications, not project references. The Our Work page distinguishes documented team research and publications from potential future assignments.

Can we request a web application or GIS dashboard?

Yes, a discovery conversation can cover users, workflow, data formats, security, integration and hosting. Any build commitment depends on agreed requirements, resources, maintenance scope and budget.

Are you hiring?

No active vacancy is advertised on this site. You may send a professional expression of interest for possible future project-based collaboration, without any guarantee of engagement.

Can the firm share its capability statement?

Yes. Open Capabilities & Delivery and use Print / Save to create a copy. If you need a formal, signed company profile or registration evidence for procurement, please request documents relevant to the particular eligibility requirement.
